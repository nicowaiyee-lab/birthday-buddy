# Birthday Buddy

Never miss a customer's birthday again. Birthday Buddy reads your customer list, flags every birthday 3 days ahead, and prepares a ready-to-send wish with a birthday card image, in English, 华语 or Bahasa Melayu.

## What it does

- **This week at a glance:** today's birthdays, the ones 3 days away (time to prepare), and the next 30 days.
- **Wishes in 3 languages and 4 styles:** Classic · 正式, Warm · 温馨, Short · 简短, Blessings · 吉祥.
- **Address customers your way:** title + full name, full name only, or their preferred name (称呼).
- **Card images:** five designs, with your logo if you add one.
- **One-tap WhatsApp:** opens the chat with the wish filled in and copies the card image. You press Send.
- **PDF list:** one page per customer with the card, the wish and a WhatsApp button.
- **Calendar reminders:** a reminder 3 days before every birthday, every year.

## How to use

1. Open the app and go to **Settings**. Add your company name and your own name.
2. Go to **Import list** and choose your Excel, CSV, PDF or photo of the customer list. Check any rows marked orange or red, then save.
3. Every Monday, open **This week**. Tap **WhatsApp** on each customer, paste the card image, and press Send.

Your file needs a heading row. Columns: Customer Name, Title, Preferred Name, Company, Birthday, Phone, Email, Account Manager. Title and Preferred Name are optional.

## Privacy

The app runs entirely in your browser. Customer lists are read and stored only on your own device and are never uploaded. This repository contains only the app itself, with no customer data.
