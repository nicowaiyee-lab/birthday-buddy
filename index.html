Plain-English guide

How to use Birthday Buddy

  1. Set up once. In Settings, type your company name and your own name. Add your logo if you like.
  2. Import your list. On Import list, choose your Excel, CSV, PDF or screenshot. Check the rows marked in orange or red, fix anything wrong, then tap Save customers.
  3. Turn on reminders. In Settings, add the reminders to your phone calendar. Your phone will alert you 3 days before each birthday, every year.
  4. Every Monday (or when your phone reminds you), open This week. Orange rows are birthdays 3 days away. Prepare those now.
  5. Choose your defaults. At the top of This week (or in Settings), pick the wish language (English, 华语 or Malay) and the wish style (Classic, Warm, Short, Blessings, or a mix). You can override both for any one customer.
  6. Send it yourself. Each birthday card shows the wish and the image. Tap the green WhatsApp button: the chat opens with the wish filled in and the card image is copied, so paste it in and press Send. The app never sends anything on its own.
  7. Want to change something? Tap Edit / more to pick a card design, edit the wording, save the image or email the customer.
  8. Tick it off. Tap Mark as wished so the team knows it's done.
  9. Need a printout or want to share with the team? On This week, tap Download PDF.

Rows with a missing or impossible birthday go to "Needs fixing" instead of being guessed. Phone numbers missing the leading 0 are corrected and flagged so you can check them.
